BBSUTSD CAMPUS ANDROID APP - INSTALLATION & EXPORT GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE TO GET THE WORKING INSTALLABLE APK:

1. RECOMMENDED (Directly from Google AI Studio):
   - Open the Settings (gear icon) menu in Google AI Studio.
   - Click "Generate APK" (or "Generate APK / AAB").
   - AI Studio will build and download the complete, signed ~23 MB
     installable APK directly to your device.
   * Note: AI Studio's general "Export as ZIP" button is meant for source
     code and often strips or truncates pre-built binary .apk/.zip files
     to 0 bytes during browser download. Always use "Settings -> Generate
     APK / AAB" for the APK binary!

2. FROM WORKSPACE ZIP ARCHIVE (If downloaded intact):
   - Extract `BBSUTSD_Campus_APK.zip` (at project root).
   - Inside you will find `BBSUTSD_Campus.apk` (~23 MB) and this guide.
   - If your extracted ZIP/APK shows 0 bytes, that confirms AI Studio's
     project-source ZIP stripped the binary; use Method 1 ("Settings ->
     Generate APK") or Method 3 below.

3. BUILD LOCALLY FROM SOURCE CODE:
   - Open the exported source project in Android Studio, OR run:
       gradle assembleDebug
   - Output APK location:
       app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Transfer or download `BBSUTSD_Campus.apk` (or `app-debug.apk`)
        onto your Android phone.

Step 2: Tap the `.apk` file in your phone's "Files" / "My Files" app.

Step 3: If prompted with "For your security, your phone is not allowed
        to install unknown apps from this source":
        - Tap "Settings"
        - Turn ON "Allow from this source"
        - Go back and tap "Install"

Step 4: Tap "Open" to launch BBSUTSD Campus!
